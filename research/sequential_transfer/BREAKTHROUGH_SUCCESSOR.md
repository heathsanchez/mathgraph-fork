# Research objective: a demonstrated advance in discovery

Heath Sanchez — 28 November 2026

Status: CANDIDATE experiment specification. No successor result is claimed.
This supersedes the programme's acceptance objective; it does not change the
protocols, outcomes or significance of the already completed trials.

## Outcome to establish

A frontier-model-driven system encounters an unfamiliar substantial problem,
identifies a verified limit of its current investigation method, constructs a
new executable investigation method, and uses that acquisition to improve the
construction of a later method. Those changes must produce a repeated,
independently accepted advantage on subsequent real assignments, with complete
acquisition and execution costs and no intervening human repair.

The proposed breakthrough is the demonstrated improvement of discovery itself.
A working orchestrator, another generated checker, a larger passing corpus, an
advisory memory or a new name does not establish that outcome.

## Evidence already available

P6-native repaired four of four historical tasks in every arm. All learned
guards matched; identical paired prompts shared responses. It established no
selection or comparative learning advantage. P6-context is a separate three of
four trial and cannot be pooled with it.

P7 generated a checker from a supplied contract and found no current-source
discrepancy. The competent conventional control tied. Its qualification remains
valid; it supplies no failure-driven method acquisition or discovery advantage.

The corrected Bundy trial has a stronger starting residual. Its recorded French
text follows a three-state closed orbit under the actual repeat-only executor;
the independently intended output is outside that orbit. Under the recorded
operation's determinism, no number of repeats can solve that case. This is a
limit of that executor, not of Python, text repair or all conventional methods.
Its 349 previously scored pairs and exposed residuals are development material,
never a fresh scoring cohort. Source 27047e15101a136f2ae67516f52c99b6a025c8ca,
run 46105804259, exact obstruction in the independently audited evidence packet.

## Consequential experiment

Start from that actual closed-orbit failure. A constructor receives the old
program, its native transition evidence, the independent development contract
and ordinary source/API access. It must produce an executable investigation or
repair-plan constructor that can leave the old orbit. The constructor language
must permit ordinary code, without a supplied menu containing the desired new
operation. The model's prior knowledge and the supplied substrate remain
explicit; a constructed operation is not automatically a novel algorithm.

Qualify the produced method against the development case and independently
justified preservation controls. Reject input-specific answer tables and
unsupported acceptance rules. Freeze the program before admitting future task
sources. If it cannot qualify, retain that failure and stop this route.

Require an acquisition test before a large evaluation: the acquired method
must actually change the evidence or search used to construct a successor.
Physically remove it, restore it, and replay its exact observations separately.
A missing-file refusal or a renamed reference is insufficient. If replayed raw
observations recover the successor, report mediation rather than intrinsic
artifact necessity. If there is no acquisition effect, do not fund a broad
benchmark of that claimed recursive mechanism.

Use substantial independently selected source-native assignments after the
freeze. Selection, common independent acceptance tests, stopping rules, primary
outcome, statistical analysis and complete per-arm resource budgets must be
pinned before exposure. Retain every admission failure, failed attempt,
regression and UNKNOWN. At least two later acquisition generations and multiple
independent problem families are required for the programme-level claim.

## Controls and promotion

The strong conventional investigator gets the same model, raw evidence, tools,
permissions and total resource budget. It may inspect full history, write code,
learn and reconstruct an equivalent method. Compare independent samples when
prompts differ; record prompt equality and shared responses explicitly. Match
extra attempts and feedback. Include cold acquisition, physical withdrawal and
restoration, and the exact executable ledger as distinct controls.

Exact executable replay should tie the learned system. Beating a control given
the identical executable is not a coherent criterion. The differentiating
result must be higher independently verified discovery/repair yield under
matched total budgets, or lower cumulative acquisition and execution cost for
equal quality. Account for model usage, native work, failures and human input;
do not turn incomparable resource counts into an invented economic score.

Promotion requires all of the following: a verified effective-method limit;
source-bound automatic construction; a causal improvement to subsequent method
acquisition; prospective transfer; a repeated advantage over the strong
conventional investigator; independently checked solutions and preservation;
and a frozen cold replay with evidence withdrawal. Field-level significance
also requires comparison with existing executable-skill and self-modifying
agent research and independent replication. Successful integration alone does
not establish research novelty.

Current judgement: the breakthrough is NOT ESTABLISHED. The programme now
targets that result; the previous negative comparisons remain binding evidence.
